Frequently Asked Questions

Are your puppies vaccinated? ▼

Yes. Every puppy receives age-appropriate vaccinations before going home. You'll receive a complete vaccination record and can continue with your veterinarian's recommended schedule.

Can I visit the puppies? ▼

Absolutely! Visits can be arranged by appointment to ensure the best experience for you, your family, and the puppies. This also helps us get to know families and ensure the right match.

What comes with my puppy? ▼

Each puppy comes home with: health records, vaccination certificate, care guide, starter food, comfort blanket, favourite toy, microchip information, and ongoing breeder support for life.

Do you provide after-sale support? ▼

Absolutely. We're always available to help with advice, guidance, and support after your puppy joins your family. We view this as the beginning of a lifelong relationship, not the end of a transaction.

What is your breeding philosophy? ▼

We prioritise health, temperament, and responsible breeding practices above all else. Every puppy is raised with love and early socialisation, and we carefully match puppies to families for long-term success.

Ready to Meet Your New Family Member?

We'd love to help you find the perfect puppy. Whether you have questions about available litters, upcoming puppies, or the adoption process, we're here to help every step of the way.
